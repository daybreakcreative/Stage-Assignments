Aurora worlds — v2

Now each mood is its own world: the radius (curves), buttons, full type system, surface, texture, and density all change — not just the color. Molten is unchanged. Graphite + Platinum are the new basic/textured pair. Frost and Nebula are reworked so they're structurally different from Molten, not recolors of it.

Static mockup — not the live app. Look at the corners, the buttons under each title, and the fonts (not just the headline). Scroll: 🔥 Molten → ◾ Graphite → ◽ Platinum → ❄️ Frost → 🌌 Nebula.
