Sanctuary Broadcast Aurora

Three looks, your pick.

Each is a shippable theme — a person can choose the vibe that fits their room, and every theme has a dark and light mode. Dark is the hero (most booths run dark); light adapts for a bright green room. Toggle the button up top to flip all three live. Same real roster + service order as your display.

A cathedral hush. High-contrast Cormorant serif, gold hairlines, candlelight glow, small-caps labels, centered composition. Feels sacred and considered — nothing shouts. Signature: the gold ornamental rule and italic gold IEM.
